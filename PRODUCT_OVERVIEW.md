# Voro AI: The Future of Desktop Assistance

Welcome to **Voro**, an ultra-low latency, multimodal, and screen-aware AI desktop assistant designed to revolutionize how professionals, developers, and students interact with artificial intelligence. 

Unlike traditional web-based AI chatbots that interrupt your workflow, Voro lives natively on your desktop as a transparent, always-on-top overlay. It sees what you see, hears what you say, and responds instantly—all while fiercely protecting your privacy.

---

## 🎯 The Problem Voro Solves

Today's AI tools are powerful but disconnected. If you want AI to help you debug code, analyze a spreadsheet, or answer a question based on a document, you have to:
1. Switch windows.
2. Manually take screenshots or copy-paste text.
3. Upload it to a cloud server.
4. Wait for a response.

Furthermore, if you use a voice assistant during a remote meeting (like Discord, Teams, or Google Meet), the AI's voice is broadcasted to everyone in the call, completely eliminating your privacy.

**Voro solves all of this.**

---

## 🌟 Key User Benefits

### 1. Seamless, Interruption-Free Workflow
Voro is designed as a frameless, transparent overlay. You never have to switch windows or leave your current application. Simply press a hotkey, speak your question naturally, and Voro will provide the answer right where you are working.

### 2. Intelligent Screen Awareness
Voro has "eyes." By utilizing advanced Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and multimodal vision models, Voro can actively analyze your active screen. If you have a complex graph, a block of code, or a difficult question on your screen, you can simply ask, *"Can you explain this to me?"* and Voro will instantly understand the context.

### 3. Screenshare Stealth & Total Privacy
Voro features a custom-built, hardware-routed audio engine. This means Voro's voice completely bypasses standard Windows Media Foundation capture hooks. **If you are sharing your screen in a meeting, interview, or stream, nobody else will hear Voro speak.** It is your personal, private assistant.

### 4. Uncompromising Data Security (Offline Mode)
Voro supports 100% local, offline execution. By integrating with tools like Ollama and utilizing local aster-whisper models, your voice, your screen data, and your conversations never have to leave your computer. 

### 5. Conversational Speed
Cloud voice assistants are slow. Voro utilizes ultra-optimized Voice Activity Detection (VAD) and local speech-to-text processing to achieve sub-second response times. The moment you finish speaking, Voro is already answering.
